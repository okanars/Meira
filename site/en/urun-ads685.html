About the product

An aluminium-bodied tabletop diffuser designed for commercial spaces. The light ring in its base gives it a decorative presence. Draws 12 W at DC 12V, runs at ≤35 dB and scents 400-1,200 m³ from its 200 ml reservoir. Available in white and black.

Key features

  • Commercial use
  • Aluminium body
  • Low noise (≤35 dB)
  • Tabletop placement

Questions about ADS-685

How large an area does ADS-685 scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 400-1,200 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 130-400 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How is ADS-685 powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: DC 12V / 12W.

How loud is ADS-685?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is ≤ 35 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of ADS-685 is 200 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.
