Questions about ADS-620B / 640B / 670B

How large an area does ADS-620B / 640B / 670B scent?

ADS-620B / 640B / 670B is designed for spaces of 2,000-5,000 / 4,000-8,000 / 7,000-12,000 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 665-1,665 m² / 1,330-2,665 m² / 2,330-4,000 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How is ADS-620B / 640B / 670B powered?

ADS-620B / 640B / 670B runs on DC 12V.

How loud is ADS-620B / 640B / 670B?

Its noise level in operation is ≤ 35 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of ADS-620B / 640B / 670B is 1000 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.

Can ADS-620B / 640B / 670B be connected to the HVAC system?

Yes. The connection point (return air duct or air handling unit outlet) depends on the building's mechanical installation.
