Questions about ADS-687

How large an area does ADS-687 scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 300-600 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 100-200 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does ADS-687 work?

It uses dual-fluid (compressed air) atomisation: an air stream breaks the fragrance into very fine particles. No water or heat is used.

How is ADS-687 powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: DC 6V / 2W.

How loud is ADS-687?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is ≤ 35 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of ADS-687 is 150 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.
