How does scent spread?

The electric devices in the catalogue work in two main ways: dual-fluid atomisation using compressed air, and ultrasonic atomisation. The method stated by the manufacturer is listed among the features on each product page.

Fine particles, dry surfaces

According to the manufacturer, in dual-fluid systems compressed air breaks the fragrance into micro particles, which an ionised high-speed accelerator then reduces to nano scale.

Very fine particles do not break when they hit a surface; they bounce off. Large droplets break where they land and can leave a wet residue.

In ultrasonic models (for example CK-628, CK-638, CK-648, CK-608D) a replaceable ultrasonic plate turns the fragrance into mist.

Scheduling and intensity from your phone

On app-enabled models, operating hours, spray interval and scent intensity are set from a phone. The connection is Bluetooth or WiFi depending on the model.

Connecting to HVAC and fresh-air systems

In large spaces the device connects to the building's ventilation duct; scent spreads through the vents with the airflow.
