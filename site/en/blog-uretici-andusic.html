Meet our manufacturer: Andusic and its production facility in Dongguan

The scent diffusers on our site are designed and made by Dongguan Andusic Technology Co. Ltd. (Andusic), the manufacturer we distribute in Türkiye. In this article we introduce Andusic: its facility, product groups, quality processes and partnerships, based on the company's own presentation.

A manufacturer based in Dongguan

Andusic was founded in Dongguan, one of China's major manufacturing centres. Since 2018 it has been developing hygiene and scent solutions for commercial spaces, hotels and public-health facilities, and describes itself as a technology and system-solution provider for hygiene, environment, health and smart living. The company stresses that it is a manufacturer, not a trading company: design, production, testing and shipment all take place under one roof.

Andusic in numbers

The figures shared in the manufacturer's presentation are:

  • 21 years of manufacturing experience
  • 20,000 m² of production area under roof
  • Annual output capacity of more than 2 million sets
  • Exports to more than 80 countries
  • More than 10,000 corporate clients
  • More than 100 patents and inventions

Two product groups

Andusic aims to supply what commercial facilities need from a single source:

  • Fragrance series: essential-oil and aroma diffusers with matching fragrances. The products Meira offers in Türkiye come from this series; see the wall-mounted, HVAC-connected and tabletop models in the product catalogue.
  • Washroom series: soap dispensers, hand dryers and towel dispensers. We prepare quotes for these products on request.

Four steps from order to shipment

  1. Confirmation: the order is confirmed, production documents are prepared and materials are planned.
  2. Production: materials are prepared and assembled; products go through ageing and performance tests.
  3. Quality: in-process inspection is followed by a final inspection, and the products are packed.
  4. Delivery: products are loaded and shipped by container.

Quality and compliance

According to the manufacturer, the products comply with CE and RoHS, and a safety data sheet (MSDS) is provided for the fragrances. Quality is checked at every stage: material sourcing, processing, testing and packing. You can request the relevant documents during the quote process.

OEM / ODM partnerships

In its presentation, the manufacturer states that it has developed co-branded (OEM / ODM) products with organisations such as BYD, TCL, Skyworth, ICBC, Alibaba Group, OCT, JLL and COCO Park, as well as hospitals, universities and property groups. For logo printing, colours, packaging or a custom scent for your own brand, please use the OEM / ODM form; we pass your request to the manufacturer and follow the process on your behalf.

Meira's role

As the Turkish distributor of Andusic's fragrance products, Meira handles product selection, quotes and supply. To find the right device for your space, try the volume calculator or contact us through the quote form.
