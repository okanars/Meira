Frequently asked questions

Here are the most common questions about choosing a product, the quote process and everyday use. If you don't find your answer, write to us through the quote form.

Quotes and purchasing

Can I buy online?

No. Products are sold through business quotes; there are no prices, payments or accounts on the site. Add the products you are interested in to the quote list and request a quote with a single form.

How do I request a quote?

Build your quote list with the "Add to list" button next to products in the catalogue. Open the list with the "Quote list" button at the top right and choose "Transfer to quote form"; products and quantities are added to the message automatically. If you also describe the size and use of the space, the quote is prepared accordingly.

Can I get samples?

Our fragrance collection is being prepared. For a scent sample, choose "Price and samples" in the quote form and describe your space and the character of scent you are looking for; sample terms are given with the quote.

Can I have products made with my own brand's logo?

The manufacturer states that it offers private-label production (OEM) and joint product development (ODM): logo printing on the device body, colour choice, custom packaging and a fragrance formula made for your brand. We pass your request to the manufacturer and follow the process on your behalf. Minimum order and lead times are given per request.

Choosing a device

Which device suits my space?

The first criterion is volume: floor area x ceiling height. Choose a model whose coverage meets that volume. The volume calculator (Solutions page) does this calculation and lists suitable models. Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation and heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

Why is coverage given in m³ rather than m²?

Scent fills a volume of air, not a floor. Of two spaces with the same floor area, the one with the higher ceiling has a larger volume and needs a stronger device. For a few tabletop models the manufacturer gives coverage in m²; this is stated on their product pages.

What is the difference between ultrasonic and dual-fluid devices?

Ultrasonic devices turn fragrance into mist with a vibrating plate; they are very quiet and suit small spaces. Dual-fluid (compressed air) devices break the fragrance into very fine particles with an air stream; they cover larger spaces and some models connect to the HVAC system.

How loud are the devices?

According to the manufacturer, the ultrasonic wall models (CK-628, CK-638, CK-648) run below 8 dBA. Dual-fluid and professional models range from about 35 to 42 dB depending on the model. Each model's value is on its own product page.

Which models can be connected to the HVAC system?

According to the manufacturer, CK-631, ADS-620B / 640B / 670B, CK-611 / 612 / 613 and CK-621 / 622 / 623 support HVAC connection, and the ADS-687 column diffuser supports connection to fresh-air systems. The connection point depends on the building's mechanical installation.

Use and fragrances

How often do I need to refill?

Refill frequency depends on the reservoir capacity, operating hours, spray and pause times and the chosen intensity. Larger reservoirs and timed operation reduce how often you refill. We can estimate consumption together for your usage plan during the quote.

Why does the scent seem to fade during the day?

People quickly get used to a scent they are constantly exposed to. Those who spend a long time in the space notice it less, while someone arriving notices it clearly. Before increasing the intensity, ask someone who has just come in.

Are the fragrances safe?

According to the manufacturer, its fragrances have been tested by SGS for compliance with IFRA standards. You can request the safety data sheet (SDS) and compliance documents during the quote process. We recommend not setting the intensity higher than needed and making sure the space is ventilated.

How are wall-mounted devices installed?

According to the manufacturer, the CK-628, CK-638 and CK-688 wall models mount with adhesive in about 3 seconds, without drilling. The plug-in CK-644 and CK-645 need no drilling. The mounting method of other models is confirmed during the quote.
