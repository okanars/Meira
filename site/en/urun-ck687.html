Questions about ADS-686

How large an area does ADS-686 scent?

ADS-686 is designed for spaces of 300-600 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 100-200 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does ADS-686 work?

It uses dual-fluid (compressed air) atomisation: an air stream breaks the fragrance into very fine particles. No water or heat is used.

How is ADS-686 powered?

ADS-686 runs on DC 6V / 2W.

How loud is ADS-686?

Its noise level in operation is ≤ 35 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of ADS-686 is 150 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.
