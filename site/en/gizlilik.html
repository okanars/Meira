Personal data processed

When you fill in the quote and contact form, only the information you share in the form is processed:

  • Identity: first and last name.
  • Contact: email address, phone number (optional).
  • Customer transaction: organisation or company name, subject, message and the products and quantities in your quote list.
  • Transaction security: the IP address and time the request was sent (to prevent misuse).

The form does not ask for special categories of personal data (health, beliefs and so on); please do not share such information in the message field.

Purposes and legal grounds

Your personal data is processed to:

  • assess your quote request and prepare a quote with price and delivery details,
  • contact you about your request, sample, OEM / ODM or technical support enquiry,
  • prevent misuse of the form and unwanted submissions.

This processing is based on Article 5(2) of KVKK: it is directly related to the conclusion of a contract (the quote process) and the legitimate interest of the data controller (information security).

Transfers

Your personal data may be shared only for the purposes above and only as far as necessary:

  • Service providers: companies that provide the website's hosting and email services.
  • Manufacturer: private-label (OEM / ODM) or product-specific technical requests may be passed to the manufacturer abroad (China) so that they can be fulfilled. Such transfers follow the conditions in Article 9 of KVKK and are limited to the information needed for your request.
  • Authorities: competent public institutions where there is a legal obligation.

Retention

Your personal data is kept for as long as the purpose of processing requires and for the periods set by the relevant legislation; at the end of these periods it is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you have the right to apply to the data controller to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose,
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  • request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate, request deletion or destruction under the conditions in Article 7 of KVKK, and request that these actions be notified to third parties to whom it was transferred,
  • object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems,
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

How to apply

You can send requests about your rights by email to info@meirascent.com or in writing, together with information that verifies your identity, in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. Applications are concluded within 30 days at the latest.

Cookies and browser storage

This site does not use cookies and contains no visitor tracking or advertising analytics. Your browser's local storage is used for the following so that the site works:

  • Quote list: the products and quantities you add to the list (localStorage). This stays only in your browser and is sent as part of the message when you submit the quote form.
  • Theme preference: if you choose the dark theme, that preference (localStorage); on later visits the site opens in the theme you chose.
  • Opening animation: a flag (sessionStorage) so that the home page opening animation is shown once per session; it is deleted when the browser tab is closed.

You can delete this information at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
