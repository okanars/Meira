About the product

High mist-volume technology that works within seconds. Stands on the floor and can be connected to the central air-conditioning (HVAC) system. One-touch control from the smart app, CNC display and adjustable scent intensity. Very quiet operation; plug-in atomiser head and magnetic lock for easy maintenance.

Questions about ADS-620B / 640B / 670B

How large an area does ADS-620B / 640B / 670B scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 2,000-5,000 / 4,000-8,000 / 7,000-12,000 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 665-1,665 m² / 1,330-2,665 m² / 2,330-4,000 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How is ADS-620B / 640B / 670B powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: DC 12V.

How loud is ADS-620B / 640B / 670B?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is ≤ 35 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of ADS-620B / 640B / 670B is 1000 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.

Can ADS-620B / 640B / 670B be connected to the HVAC system?

According to the manufacturer, yes. The connection point (return air duct or air handling unit outlet) depends on the building's mechanical installation.
