About the product

An innovative diffuser with a built-in air pump that can be integrated into HVAC systems. Operating hours and scent intensity are set precisely from a phone. Offers infrared oil-level sensing and cold-air diffusion. Food-grade PP; FDA compliant according to the manufacturer.

Key features

  • Bluetooth / WiFi remote control
  • Very strong atomisation
  • Built-in air pump
  • 24-hour smart timer
  • HVAC connection
  • Infrared oil-level sensing
  • Cold-air diffusion

Questions about CK-631

How large an area does CK-631 scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 3,500 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 1,165 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How is CK-631 powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: DC 12V / 12W.

How loud is CK-631?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is < 42 dBA.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of CK-631 is 800 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.

Can CK-631 be connected to the HVAC system?

According to the manufacturer, yes. The connection point (return air duct or air handling unit outlet) depends on the building's mechanical installation.
