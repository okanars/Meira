How to read a scent pyramid

Every fragrance is built from three layers of notes that complete one another. The pyramid describes the scent from the first notes you notice to the trace it leaves.

  1. Top notes

    The first impression. The light, volatile notes you notice as you walk in: citrus, green leaves, fresh aromatics.

  2. Heart notes

    The character. The flowers, fruits and spices that give the fragrance its identity.

  3. Base notes

    The lasting trace. Musk, amber, woods and vanilla that give the scent depth and continuity.

Diffusers release the fragrance into the air as very fine particles without heating it, so the whole scent is present in the room. You set the intensity with the device's spray and pause times.
