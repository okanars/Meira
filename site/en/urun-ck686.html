About the product

A free-standing commercial column diffuser that makes a design statement with its stone-textured atomiser head. Plug-in atomiser for easy oil changes and a magnetic lock. One-touch control from the smart app with LED display. Nano mist technology for 1,500-2,000 m³; timed operation in WiFi and Bluetooth modes, and connects to fresh-air systems.

Questions about ADS-687

How large an area does ADS-687 scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 1,500-2,000 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 500-665 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does ADS-687 work?

The manufacturer states that this model uses nano atomisation: the fragrance is released into the air as very fine particles.

How is ADS-687 powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: DC 12V / 12W.

How loud is ADS-687?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is ≤ 35 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of ADS-687 is 500 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.

Can ADS-687 be connected to the HVAC system?

According to the manufacturer, yes. The connection point (return air duct or air handling unit outlet) depends on the building's mechanical installation.
