About the product

Turns fragrance into a fine mist by ultrasonic atomisation and is managed from a Bluetooth mobile app. The PP-bodied unit draws 2 W at DC 6V, runs at ≤35 dB and scents 300-800 m³ from its 300 ml reservoir. Available in white and black.

Key features

  • Ultrasonic atomisation
  • Bluetooth app control
  • Low noise (≤35 dB)
  • 300 ml fragrance reservoir

Questions about CK-629

How large an area does CK-629 scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 300-800 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 100-265 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does CK-629 work?

It uses ultrasonic atomisation: a plate vibrating at high frequency turns the fragrance into a fine mist. No heat is used.

How is CK-629 powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: DC 6V / 2W.

How loud is CK-629?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is ≤ 35 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of CK-629 is 300 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.
