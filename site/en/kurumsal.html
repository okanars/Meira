Distribution and manufacturer

Meira is the Turkish distributor of scent diffusers and fragrances made by Andusic (Dongguan Andusic Technology Co. Ltd.). It handles product selection, quotes and supply for projects in Türkiye.

Manufacturer: Andusic

The products are designed and made by Dongguan Andusic Technology Co. Ltd. (Andusic), based in Dongguan, one of China's major manufacturing centres. Founded in 2018, the company develops smart hygiene and scent solutions for commercial spaces, hotels and public-health facilities.

Andusic is a manufacturer, not a trading company: it runs the whole process, from design to shipment, in its own facility.

Production under one roof

The production line, CNC and injection moulding workshops, testing area and finished goods warehouse are all in the same facility in Dongguan. From there, products are shipped to more than 80 countries across Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas.

The corporate scent process

We define the scent of a space together in four steps.

  1. Survey

    We note the size of the space, the ceiling height, the airflow and how busy it is.

  2. Choosing a scent

    Scent samples are prepared to suit your brand's tone and your guests.

  3. Pilot installation

    The device is installed in the space and we assess the spread and intensity together.

  4. Refills and maintenance

    A regular plan is set up for fragrance refills and device maintenance.
