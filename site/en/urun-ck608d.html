Questions about CK-608D

How large an area does CK-608D scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 100 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 30 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does CK-608D work?

It uses ultrasonic atomisation: a plate vibrating at high frequency turns the fragrance into a fine mist. No heat is used.

How is CK-608D powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: DC5V-0.5A / 2.5W.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of CK-608D is 5 - 20 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.
