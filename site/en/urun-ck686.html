Questions about ADS-686 column

How large an area does ADS-686 column scent?

ADS-686 column is designed for spaces of 4,000 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 1,330 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does ADS-686 column work?

It uses nano atomisation: the fragrance is released into the air as very fine particles.

How is ADS-686 column powered?

ADS-686 column runs on DC 12V / 12W.

How loud is ADS-686 column?

Its noise level in operation is ≤ 35 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of ADS-686 column is 1000 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.

Can ADS-686 column be connected to the HVAC system?

Yes. The connection point (return air duct or air handling unit outlet) depends on the building's mechanical installation.
