Questions about CK-644

How large an area does CK-644 scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 100-300 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 30-100 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does CK-644 work?

It uses dual-fluid (compressed air) atomisation: an air stream breaks the fragrance into very fine particles. No water or heat is used.

How is CK-644 powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: DC 6V / 5W.

How loud is CK-644?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is ≤ 35 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of CK-644 is 100 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.
