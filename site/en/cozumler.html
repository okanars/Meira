Other uses

Shops and showrooms

Recommended models: 600-12,000 m³

In clothing and accessory shops it matters that scent leaves no residue on the products. According to the manufacturer, dual-fluid models leave no wet residue. The series comes in three capacities to suit the size of the showroom.

ADS-620B / 640B / 670B, ADS-689, ADS-687 column

Offices and meeting rooms

Recommended models: 300-800 m³

In meeting rooms and workspaces the noise of the device matters. According to the manufacturer, ADS-687 and ADS-689 run at 35 dB or below; their operating hours are set from a Bluetooth app.

ADS-687, ADS-689

Airports, convention centres, shopping centres

Recommended models: 4,000-15,000 m³
