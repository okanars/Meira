Questions about CK-683

How large an area does CK-683 scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 300 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 100 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does CK-683 work?

It uses dual-fluid (compressed air) atomisation: an air stream breaks the fragrance into very fine particles. No water or heat is used.

How is CK-683 powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: DC5V-2A (Type-C) / 8W.

How loud is CK-683?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is ≤ 38 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of CK-683 is 110 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.
