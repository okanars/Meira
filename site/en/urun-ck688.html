Questions about CK-688

How large an area does CK-688 scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 800 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 265 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does CK-688 work?

It uses dual-fluid (compressed air) atomisation: an air stream breaks the fragrance into very fine particles. No water or heat is used.

How is CK-688 powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: 4 × AA batteries / Type-C 6V.

How loud is CK-688?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is < 36 dB.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of CK-688 is 500 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.
