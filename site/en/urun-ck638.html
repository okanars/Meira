About the product

CK-638 spreads scent quickly by ultrasonic atomisation and comes with a smart remote, LED light sensing and an LCD control panel. According to the manufacturer, its food-grade, corrosion-resistant PP is FDA compliant. Installs in 3 seconds with an adhesive pad that leaves no marks.

Questions about CK-638

How large an area does CK-638 scent?

According to the manufacturer, its coverage is 300 m³. With a 3 m ceiling this corresponds to a floor area of about 100 m². Partitioned spaces, strong ventilation or heavy visitor traffic may need more than one unit.

How does CK-638 work?

It uses ultrasonic atomisation: a plate vibrating at high frequency turns the fragrance into a fine mist. No heat is used.

How is CK-638 powered?

According to the manufacturer, the power supply is: 4 × AA batteries / Type-C 6V.

How loud is CK-638?

According to the manufacturer, the noise level is < 8 dBA.

What is the fragrance capacity?

The fragrance capacity of CK-638 is 168 ml. How often it needs refilling depends on operating hours, spray and pause times and the intensity setting.
